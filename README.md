# Tune in to the electromagnetic frequencies around you.

The EMF Explorer Badge is a hands-on soldering kit that turns the invisible electrical activity of everyday electronics into sound. Walk around, listen, compare devices, and build intuition for the signals hiding in the modern world. Available as a soldering kit for at-home assembly or during a workshop, the EMF Explorer was designed for mass deployment at large-scale workshops. No prior electronics knowledge is necessary to participate and learn from this approachable device.

### Mass deployable
Designed to scale up to 50 students at a time - taught to hundreds of participants at events around the world.

### Travels well
Workshop is adaptable for hackerspaces, museums, classrooms, makerspaces, and large festivals - anywhere with tables and outlets.

### Take-home kit
Every participant builds and keeps their own EMF Explorer Badge - a souvenir that keeps teaching after the workshop ends.
